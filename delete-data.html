Delete your data

Mathonauts, by Olumide Oni · last updated 14 November 2026

Support · Privacy · Delete data

Mathonauts has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Everything the app knows lives on your own device. The one exception is a backup, which a grown-up can create to move progress to another device. This page is about deleting that backup.

Delete it from inside the app

This is the quickest way and it takes effect immediately.

  • Open Mathonauts.
  • Go to the Parent Zone and enter your parent PIN.
  • Tap Move progress to another device.
  • Tap Delete backup, and confirm.

The stored copy is erased straight away. Nothing is kept behind, and no copy is archived.

Ask us to delete it instead

If you no longer have the app installed, or you would rather we did it, email olutaiwo.oni@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Mathonauts backup and include your ten-character recovery code. We will delete it and reply to confirm, normally within 7 days and at most within 30.

We need the code because it is the only thing that identifies a backup — there is no name, email address or account attached to one. Without it we have no way to tell which stored block is yours. If you have lost the code, the backup cannot be identified, read or recovered by anyone, including us, and it will be deleted automatically as described below.

What is deleted

The whole backup, which is the encrypted copy of what the app holds:

  • Explorer names
  • Lesson progress, stars, XP, levels, badges and mistake history
  • Gems, outfits and items bought in the shop
  • App settings, and the one-way fingerprint of each profile's PIN

All of it is stored as a single block of AES-256-GCM ciphertext under an identifier that is a one-way hash of your recovery code. We cannot read any of it.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is kept after deletion. There is no retention period, no grace period and no backup of the backup.
  • Automatic deletion. Any backup that has not been written to for one year is deleted automatically, whether or not you ask.
  • On your device. Deleting a backup does not touch the app on your device. To remove that too: delete an explorer in the Parent Zone to erase one child's progress, or uninstall the app to remove everything it holds. Neither is recoverable.

We never collected an email address, phone number, location, device identifier or advertising identifier, so there is none to delete. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
